01 · The brief

Come for a roll. Stay for the evening.

I designed a concept website for Oshi Nori, a sushi counter in Yaletown, Vancouver. Hand rolls, flame-finished sushi and Japanese drinks, set in a dark, warm room under the koi.

The site moves like an evening at the counter: welcome, menu, the roll, the fire, the pour, then a seat to reserve.
