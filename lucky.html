01 · The brief

He loses everything. Finds LUCKY. Wins it all back.

A concept promo for Lucky Energy’s hero can, OG Luck: an open-world, game-inspired spot that plays like a cut-scene. Higher fidelity, brighter colour and more life than the game spots it nods to, but always on-brand, and built from scratch with original characters, locations and props.

I took it from a first-pass edit to a faster, 27-frame V2 storyboard, with Matrix-style slow motion, a 360° turntable and an outfit-swap gag, ending in the sun on the beach.
