01 · The brief

A pharma spot, before the shoot.

I designed and delivered two :60 animatic spots for DUPIXENT (dupilumab), working with the Toronto production company Paper Route. Every storyboard frame, character and location was designed and rendered as an AI-generated motion shot. There was no live shoot and no recording.

It ran as a 7-day rush, with revisions built into the week and a number of rounds and re-shoots along the way, so the agency could see the spots move before committing to production.
